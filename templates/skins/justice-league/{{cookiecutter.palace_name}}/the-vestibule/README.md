## What This Is

**{{ cookiecutter.palace_name }}** is an OpenLoci palace wearing the **Justice League** skin over the **Clue** chassis.

{{ cookiecutter.project_description }}

The DM is the LLM. The player is the investigator. The League assembles when called.

DC maps to the Greek pantheon: archetypes before personalities, principles before people. Superman is not Clark Kent with powers — he is the ideal of moral clarity under pressure. Batman is not Bruce Wayne with trauma — he is the will that refuses to accept that evil wins. Each character is a *force* wearing a face, which is why they endure.
